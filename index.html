What is Campus Connect?

USM Campus Connect 2026 is a student-focused awareness campaign designed for first- and second-year students. With so many activities taking place, event details and updates can be easy to miss. Our goal is to make information easier to find and encourage more students to participate.

Frequently asked questions

Who can participate?

This campaign is primarily intended for first- and second-year students. Individual event eligibility may vary; check the event description.

How do I register?

Go to Upcoming Events, select an event and use its registration link. Complete the form as instructed by the organiser.

Where can I find changes to event details?

Check the Latest Announcements section and any official organiser communications for updates.

What if an event is not listed?

Only confirmed events supplied to this website are displayed. Check back for new listings.
